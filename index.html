Share Lock

Threshold secret sharing over GF(2^8) with per-share commitments. A corrupted share is not just detected: it is named. This page renders one recorded ritual, not a simulation.

Ritual-

Tampering with share 2 changes one bit. Verification fails for exactly that index, and reconstruction over a quorum containing it returns a different secret - which is why the commitment exists.

Below threshold, nothing is learned

Three different hypothesised secrets, each extended with the same two observed shares, produce polynomials that agree with both observations and disagree about every further coordinate. A holder of two shares cannot choose between them.
